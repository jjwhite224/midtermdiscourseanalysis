04 / Methods

What the model can—and cannot—say

01 / Gather

Timestamp, not just title

A video's date alone cannot tell us when a comment was made. The collection script checks each comment's original publication time against an Eastern-time election phase.

02 / Compare

Shared vocabulary space

One TF-IDF representation is fitted across the entire pilot sample, then K-Means groups comments by word patterns. Aggregate results can be compared across both channels and all three phases.

03 / Qualify

Not a public-opinion poll

Comments are self-selected and moderated; posts may be deleted or unavailable. Upload timing and uneven sample sizes may influence what we see. Clusters do not indicate individual political beliefs.
